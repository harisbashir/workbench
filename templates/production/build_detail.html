How this works

  • Parts are marked on the BOM as fitted by the assembly house or in house (usually through-hole connectors, or parts they don't stock).
  • The order files leave the in-house parts out, so the assembler places everything else.
  • When the boards arrive, the in-house parts become this checklist. Tap +1 as each board is done — anyone on the project can, from a phone at the bench.
