Timesheets & weekly report

Logging time

  • On any task page, use the Time box: enter hours (in quarter hours, e.g. 1.5), the date, and an optional note.
  • Or open Timesheet to see your week day by day and add entries for any project.
  • Made a mistake? Delete the entry from your timesheet and add it again.

Log time at the end of each working day. It takes a minute, and your lead then doesn't need to ask for a status update.

Weekly report

The Weekly report gathers one week of activity on a single page:

  • Hours by person and project
  • Tasks finished, and tasks sent for review
  • What's blocked and what's overdue right now
  • Builds completed, with yield

Leads and administrators see the whole team; everyone else sees their own hours. Use Print / save PDF to send it on, or Hours CSV to open the hours in Excel, for example for invoicing or payroll.

Blocked tasks

If you can't make progress, edit the task and fill in Blocked by, for example “waiting for USBLC6 parts” or “needs PWR-3 first”. The project lead is notified, and the task shows in red on the board, on Home and in the weekly report. Clear the field once you're moving again.
