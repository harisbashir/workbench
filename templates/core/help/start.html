Getting started

{{ COMPANY_NAME }} Workbench is the one place for the engineering team's work: tasks, design reviews, chat, parts, BOMs, purchasing and production. It's linked to GitHub, where firmware and KiCad files live.

The main areas

AreaUse it for
HomeWhat needs you today: your tasks, reviews waiting on you, overdue work, low stock.
My workEvery unfinished task you're doing or reviewing, in one list.
ProjectsOne per product or board. Each has a task board, revisions (Rev A, Rev B…) and a team.
ChatProject channels (created automatically), topic channels, and direct messages.
GitHubOpen pull requests across projects and how they link to tasks.
Parts & BOMThe shared parts library, stock levels, suppliers and each revision's bill of materials.
ProductionBuilds (production runs) and purchase orders.

Your first day

  1. Set your profile. Add your time zone and GitHub username under Profile. Times are then shown in your local time, and your pull requests are credited to you.
  2. Open your project's board. Go to Projects and choose Board. Drag a task to In progress when you start it.
  3. Join the conversation. Your project's channel is in Chat. GitHub activity is posted there too.
  4. Name branches after tasks. Use the task ID in your branch, like pwr-12-usb-esd. That's all it takes to link your work.

Working across time zones

The team spans Pakistan and Canada (about 9 hours apart), so most work happens asynchronously:

  • Write decisions and review feedback as task comments, not only in chat — comments stay with the work.
  • Before you log off, move tasks to the right column and leave a comment saying where you got to.
  • Use @username when you need someone specific; they get a notification.

Finding things

The search bar at the top searches tasks, projects, parts (by value, MPN or footprint) and chat. Type a task ID like PWR-12 to jump to it.
