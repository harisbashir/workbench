Parts & BOMs

The parts library

Every component the company uses has one entry with its own part number (for example RES-0001), value, KiCad footprint, manufacturer and manufacturer part number (MPN), preferred supplier, unit cost, stock level and storage location.

Set a reorder level on parts you use often. When stock falls below it, the part is highlighted and counted on the Home page.

Importing a BOM from KiCad

  1. Open the project and choose the revision's BOM button.
  2. Choose Import from KiCad.
  3. Upload the CSV:
    • KiCad: Tools → Generate Bill of Materials, export as CSV.
    • KiBot: the BOM CSV from your GitHub Actions run.
  4. Check the preview. Each row is matched to the library by part number, then MPN, then value + footprint. Rows that don't match become new parts.
  5. Choose Save BOM.

Tip: add an MPN field to your KiCad symbols. Imports then match reliably, and purchasing knows exactly what to buy.

Reading a BOM page

  • Cost per board — the sum of fitted parts at their current unit cost (excluding PCB fab and assembly unless you add them as parts).
  • Boards buildable from stock — how many complete boards current stock covers, and which parts limit it.
  • Red rows — not enough stock for one board. Amber rows — below reorder level.
  • Compare with… — shows parts added, removed and changed between revisions.

Stock

Stock changes automatically when a purchase order is received (+) and when a build starts (−). Procurement can also adjust stock by hand on the part page, for example after a stock count. Every change is recorded in the part's stock history.
